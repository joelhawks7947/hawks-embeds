Where do I park?

There are around 1,200 parking spaces within a short walk of WIN Entertainment Centre, and special event rates apply at some car parks. Closest to the venue: Stewart Street car parks, Salvation Army car park, St Francis Xavier Cathedral (limited spaces) and Woolworths car park, Burelli Street. In the city centre: Market Street, Wollongong Central, Crown Central (north and south) and Wollongong Station car park. Parking on the street? Check the signs before you walk away.

What can I bring in? What's not allowed?

WIN Entertainment Centre restricts items that could cause injury, nuisance or inconvenience. Prohibited items include glass, drink bottles or cans; alcohol; aerosols; bags larger than 30 cm x 40 cm; commercial food; fireworks, weapons and laser pointers; noise-making devices; scooters and similar wheeled items; confetti or glitter; commercial recording equipment, selfie sticks, drones and oversized lenses; and oversized banners or flags. Umbrellas cannot be used in the auditorium and can be stored at external doors. Security may inspect bags and patrons on entry, and event-specific conditions may also apply.

More info: WIN Entertainment Centre conditions of entry (opens in a new tab)

Can I bring my own food or water?

Commercial food and alcohol cannot be brought into the venue. You may bring one factory-sealed, non-alcoholic PET bottle under 600 mL; other drink bottles and cans are prohibited. Event-specific conditions may also apply.

More info: WIN Entertainment Centre conditions of entry (opens in a new tab)

What food and drink is available?

Game night starts long before tip-off. Everything sits inside the Lower Crown Quarter, with the ocean at the end of the street and food and drinks a few minutes from your seat.

Inside the venue: Bars and canteens run throughout WIN Entertainment Centre. Grab food early, because queues build the closer it gets to tip-off.

Is the venue cashless?

WIN Entertainment Centre and WIN Stadium are cashless venues. All transactions will be by contactless card. Cash cards will no longer be available for purchase at outlets.

Can I leave and come back in?

Generally, no. Pass-outs for re-entry are not issued unless the specific event conditions expressly allow them.

When should I be in my seat?

Be seated at least 30 minutes before tip-off. That's when pregame entertainment starts.

Accessibility

How do I book wheelchair or accessible seating?

Easy-access and wheelchair seating is limited and must be booked before the event. Call the WSEC Ticketmaster Box Office on 02 4220 2884 during business hours to book.

Do you accept the Companion Card?

Yes. Wheelchair-accessible and Companion Card seating is available for Illawarra Hawks games through Ticketmaster's interactive section map. Select Accessible Seating from the price categories. Your Companion Card number may be required when booking; for help, contact the WSEC Ticketmaster Box Office on 02 4220 2884.

Is there accessible parking and drop-off?

Limited reserved accessible parking is available in the WSEC car park area and Quilkey Place and must be booked in advance. Call the WSEC Ticketmaster Box Office on 02 4220 2884; barrier-free entry is available from the Crown Street and Harbour Street entrances.

Is there lift access to all seating levels?

WIN Entertainment Centre is equipped with lifts and ramps where required, with accessible seating available on both main seating levels. Because venue configurations vary and accessible spaces are limited, book easy-access seating in advance through the WSEC Ticketmaster Box Office on 02 4220 2884.

Are assistance animals allowed?

Yes. Certified service animals are allowed; other animals and pets are prohibited.

Kids and families

Can I bring a pram?

Prams and strollers cannot be taken into WIN Entertainment Centre for safety reasons. They may be left in the venue's designated stroller bays; the venue is not responsible for loss, theft or damage.

What's on for kids before the game?

Plenty. The i98FM Street Fleet is onsite at every home game with games and prizes before the doors open, so come early. Inside, the free Activation Zone has Greater Bank mini hoops, the vertical jump tester and our measure-up wall. TomaHawk is out before every home game for photos.
